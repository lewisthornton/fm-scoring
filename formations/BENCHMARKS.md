# League reference distributions (version 1)

The watcher captures aggregate references and the bridge publishes `league_benchmarks.json` separately from existing scoring, recruitment and tactical outputs.

## Detection

The fmsave contracts are club/nation IDs, competition `id` (save-local) and `database_id` (editor ID), live league tables and managed-club fixtures (`competition_id`, `season_start_year`, `date`, club UIDs and team slots). See [fmsave models](https://github.com/rhiever/fmsave/tree/main/src/fmsave/models) and `leagues/divisions.toml`. A reliable league/cup discriminator or competition name is not guaranteed; fmsave itself infers table links from fixtures.

Require a unique competition record and at least eight distinct domestic clubs including the managed club, each resolving to exactly one club record in the managed league nation. All relevant fixtures must be first-team, every opponent must be seen both home and away, with at least 120 days between the first and last fixtures. Season comes from fixture start year; current date must be within the fixture range or 31 days either side. Combine valid split tables sharing the competition ID; a complete table union must equal the calendar's inferred membership.

**Preseason fallback:** when a table is absent or a valid table union is a subset of the reciprocal calendar, infer membership from that calendar. Require at least 180 days of fixtures, distinct fixture dates, uniform meetings per opponent (at least two), and home/away counts differing by at most one per opponent. Require a unique competition record with an editor database ID or explicit stage membership; if stage IDs are supplied, every fixture stage must be included. A table declaring more clubs than the calendar, conflicting membership, invalid rows, or ambiguous metadata rejects the candidate. This accepts a 33-match initial phase with 11 domestic opponents, three fixtures each, 16 home/17 away, without assuming later split fixtures or hardcoding competition IDs. Other double or multiple round-robin schedules meeting the same criteria are supported. A fixture-only irregular or partial schedule may remain unavailable even if it is a genuine league. A complete table union retains the previous 120-day threshold.

fmsave's verified `Fixture.stage_id` and `Fixture.round` (CSV `round` and `round_code`) are stronger evidence: league matchdays and phases have no round, whereas cup stages carry round codes, even if their label is UNKNOWN. Every relevant fixture must resolve a stage and explicitly export both empty round fields; missing columns, unknown/nonempty round codes and mixed round evidence fail closed. A roundless short or one-way group is not sufficient. These are conservative structural checks, not a guaranteed competition-type flag: an unusual roundless season-length cup with a complete reciprocal schedule could be indistinguishable through these fields. Unresolved domestic first-team tables containing the managed club (including small splits) block automatic selection. Multiple eligible competition/season pairs also block automatic selection; manual override must still pass all candidate gates. Offseason, incomplete schedules, cross-border leagues or smaller tables may be unsupported. No arbitrary peer fallback. Old aggregate caches must be revalidated under detection policy 3 before use; the aggregate schema and refresh cadence are unchanged.

Optional watcher `--benchmark-competition 500` selects an otherwise corroborated save-local ID when ambiguous (invented example); it cannot force an unsupported competition or ambiguous season. `--benchmark-names path/to/names.csv` maps editor IDs to display labels, columns `database_id,name` (e.g. `9000,Example Premiership`). This does not determine membership. If no name is exported or mapped, label the ID explicitly and warn.

Reuse the full-world CSV already required by selection, deleted with the other temporary files. Five scoped/metadata exports establish competition on new save hashes; repeated same-save selections can reuse the result. Refresh aggregate after 30 game days, competition/season/club-set/model/parser changes, or insufficient sample. Only the latest aggregate per career is stored in an additive SQLite table, atomically with normal acceptance. No individual reference identities or attributes are retained. Older databases return unavailable until an accepted import. Standalone installations should update both companion scripts and checkout together; watcher `--fm-scoring-root` optionally selects the checkout.

## Reference population

Use currently registered players at detected league clubs, team slot 0, unique valid UID. Loan parent is not current club; match eligibility is not inferred. Exclude duplicate IDs, unknown team slot and unknown GK type. Exact GK rating >=18 identifies goalkeeper domain. Require at least one exact rating >=15 in the relevant established profile position-code group; text-derived familiarity is not precise evidence. Versatile outfield players can appear in multiple profiles. GK/outfield distributions never mix.

Native populations additionally require the exact native position rating >=15, independently of related positions. The inherited mapping is GK:gk; FB:dl/dr/wbl/wbr; CB:dc/sw; 6:dm/mc; wing:aml/amr/ml/mr; 10:amc/mc; 9:stc. MC therefore has two distinct inherited weight-model comparisons (`6:mc` and `10:mc`), not independently calibrated weights. Native and broad populations share all quality gates; overlapping eligibility is permitted but each player is counted only once within a population. An insufficient native population never falls back to a broader percentile. Cache detection policy 5 invalidates older aggregates, including caches that may contain unavailable histograms.

Use unchanged shared weighted scoring and `fm27v2.toml`; no imputation. Require >=20 complete scores, >=4 contributing clubs and >=75% attribute coverage among confirmed familiar players per profile. These quality gates are not confidence intervals. Missing familiarity can bias samples. Quantiles interpolate at `(n-1)*p`; empirical percentile is `100*(number below + 0.5*number equal)/n`. Ties share ranks; outside observed range gives 0 or 100. Histograms are sorted aggregate score/frequency pairs, not player records. Both broad and native distributions failing any quality gate return an empty `histogram`, null quantiles and no percentile; counts, coverage, warnings and rejection reasons remain available. No match-performance probability or role-suitability estimate is implied.

## JSON contract

Root: `version` (integer), `career` (string), `game_date` (ISO date or null), `status`, `reason` (nullable string), `competition` (nullable object: IDs, display label, name source, nation IDs/code), `season_start_year` (nullable integer), `extraction_game_date` (nullable ISO date), `scoring_model` (name/hash), `criteria`, `source`, `warnings`, `provenance` (save hash, parser/build, override, optional name-map hash and independent reference hashes). Dates are in-game. Detection includes fixture counts/range, candidates and selection method. `reference` contains population/club counts and `profiles` keyed by GK, FB, CB, 6, wing, 10, 9, each with status/reasons, sample/club/eligible counts, exclusion counts, coverage (fraction), median/p25/p75/p90 (nullable numbers), histogram (`[score,count]` pairs) and warnings. Insufficient samples omit estimates.

`detection.evidence` records `detection_method` (`table_and_fixtures` or `fixture_led`), table status (`complete`, `incomplete`, `missing`), `league_tables_missing`, domestic/fixture/home/away/reciprocal counts, stage/roundless counts, calendar span and minimum/maximum meetings. `detection.method` distinguishes automatic table-led, automatic fixture-led and manual override. `detection.diagnostics` lists considered competition/season evidence and explicit rejection codes (e.g. missing metadata, non-domestic opponents, no reciprocal coverage, short calendar, table conflict, invalid stage/round or outside season window); `unresolved_managed_table` explains an additional ambiguity gate and `ignored_fixture_counts` counts unlinked, non-managed or known non-first-team fixtures. The watcher logs the selected method or rejection reasons. A fixture-led inference is not proof of the competition's official name; absent name metadata still produces an ID label, not a guessed name.

`comparisons` has one row per scoped stable player ID and profile: attribute score, familiarity status, benchmark status/reason, competition ID, season, sample size, median, signed difference and percentile (nullable numbers), warnings. No comparison unless player type, familiarity, score and distribution are supported. Earlier reference dates are flagged. Tactical fit and availability remain separate; no assignment or optimisation.

`reference.native_profiles` is keyed by `profile:native_position` and has the same aggregate statistics plus explicit population identity. `native_comparisons` has one row per player/profile/native-position combination, with `reference_population=native_position` and a single native code. `profiles` and `comparisons` remain broad (`profile_group`) for compatibility. XI and depth use the native join; signing provides both labelled broad context and `native_league_context` by pair, displaying the native percentile. Relaxed alternative eligibility alone does not establish native benchmark familiarity at the assigned selection position.

Tests use invented competitions and players; actual FM27 save extraction was not performed. Unavailable source evidence is reported explicitly while existing scoring/imports continue.

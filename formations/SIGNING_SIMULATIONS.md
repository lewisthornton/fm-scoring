# Single-signing simulations (version 1)

The bridge publishes `signing_simulations.json` and `signing_simulations.html` in the same output directory as the existing XI, depth and recruitment views. The new page links to them; the legacy recruitment ranking and native HTML remain unchanged. The array of candidates is the separate ranked shortlist; no redundant priorities file is needed.

## Hypothetical contract

`hypothetical_assessment(assessment, candidate_id)` creates a detached in-memory roster consisting of the active squad plus exactly one active target not already in the squad. Stable identities and their slot IDs are validated; malformed or conflicting career-scoped watcher keys, duplicate IDs and conflicting slot IDs are rejected. Pure clients may use opaque IDs scoped to the supplied assessment. No identities are merged by name. The candidate's local squad membership and availability (including per-slot availability) are assumed, not observed; actual club, loan state, date, familiarity and attributes remain unchanged. The original assessment and benchmark are never edited. An overlapping target/squad player is labelled `already_in_squad` with no additional-player scenario. The bridge includes only scoped matched targets, not unselected full-world records.

`build_signing_simulations(formation, assessment, benchmark=None, *, optimizer, depth_module, policy=None, baseline=None, baseline_depth=None, depth_limit=12, observations=None)` reuses the selected-checkout modules, including the exact baseline XI and depth when supplied. It verifies formation hash, career, date, model, policy, baseline identity and ordered slots. Every non-overlapping target receives a complete exact XI solve with the existing eligibility predicate and weighted scores unchanged. The solver's lexicographic objective is checked for monotonicity with exact binary-rational score comparisons and stable-ID ties. No new solver or scoring coefficients.

## Interpretation and ranking

- `completeness_upgrade`: at least one more filled slot, before any score comparison. A newly filled GK has no pre-signing GK score difference (null).
- `starting_upgrade`: unchanged cardinality and strictly better numeric lexicographic XI objective. Outfield and goalkeeper scores are separate; a tie-only reassignment is not a starting upgrade.
- `depth_improvement`: no numeric XI improvement but evaluated depth improves at least one supported measure: direct count, independent cover, shared-pair or overall distinct bench capacity, relieved sole-cover dependency, or same-player absence objective.
- `depth_not_evaluated`: eligible edges exist and XI objective did not improve, but detailed depth was outside the explicit limit. Not a negative recommendation or a completed depth result.
- `no_demonstrated_improvement`: no eligible edge with sufficient known evidence, or no supported improvement after evaluation. This is not a claim the player has no footballing value.
- `insufficient_evidence`: no eligible edge and missing compatible profile or familiarity evidence.
- `already_in_squad`: not a signing candidate.

Sort by those groups; completeness by slots gained first. Within XI improvement groups, outfield and GK candidates are grouped separately (outfield first), then by the relevant domain's score gain, not a sum of the two. Within equivalent groups prefer more original absences with rescued slots, greater distinct bench capacity gain, newly covered slots then stable ID. These are transparent priority rules, not a calibrated value function. Missing GK differences remain null. An unselected candidate cannot improve the starting XI objective. Equal-objective changes are labelled `tie_break_only` even when the candidate starts.

## Depth and performance

All targets receive XI evaluation. Full depth (including starter-loss scenarios) runs for at most `--signing-depth-limit N` candidates (default 12, nonnegative; 0 disables detailed depth only). Eligible candidates are shortlisted by the initial XI hierarchy, domain gain and stable ID; no unaudited prefilter removes targets. Candidates outside the limit retain exact XI results and explicit `not_evaluated_limit`; candidates with no eligible edge receive `not_applicable_no_eligible_slot`. Detailed depth uses Milestone 10 on the new squad and compares direct bench IDs/counts, newly covered and independent slots, shared-pair distinct-player capacity, overall bench capacity and relieved sole-cover dependencies. Compare the exact best XI with and without a **fixed original starter ID**. If that starter is still in the new XI, reuse the new depth event; if displaced, solve another exclusion using the same original ID. New-XI starter-loss events are separately labelled. Never infer resilience from a changed loss delta relative to different baselines.

All scenarios are local reads of the already scoped assessments. No world export, database import or attribute rescoring per target; the depth limit bounds repeated expensive solves. The test suite includes 30 targets with a limit of two and reversed-order parity. A dense 35-player/60-target diagnostic with a depth limit of three completed in approximately 15.8 seconds locally (including assessments and baseline depth). Runtime varies by squad size and position versatility; larger or denser cohorts can lower the limit without losing any XI evaluations.

## JSON and presentation

Root: version, career/game date, formation/model provenance, effective policy and objective, baseline file references and totals, explicit assumptions, depth limit/evaluated count, ranking rationale, stable-ID/name directory and ordered candidates. Each candidate has observed identity, club, availability, loan/cohort state, observation dates/build, optional observed attributes and raw contract/value/loan metadata in original export units, observed slot evidence and hypothetical availability. `eligible_slot_ids` and `rejected_slots` are scenario-specific; no missing data is imputed. `league_context` reuses the existing career/date/model and reference freshness checks, reference sample and warnings. All percentile/difference data are contextual only. Value is not asking price; wages/fees are not forecast and no currency conversion occurs.

`xi` records baseline and hypothetical totals, objective impact, actual selected slot, its original occupant, changed and newly filled/vacant slots, moved starters and newly benched IDs, and compact ordered assignments with warnings. `depth` has explicit status and, when evaluated, coverage and capacity deltas, relieved dependencies, same-original-ID absence outcomes and the new team's own starter-loss scenarios. Compact records reference stable IDs and existing player evidence rather than copying entire assessments per absence. The read-only escaped HTML compares baseline and resulting XI, eligibility, league context and expandable changes/depth details.

This is an uncalibrated attribute-based counterfactual, not a measured match outcome, real signing recommendation, budget or prediction of transfer feasibility. No actual transfers, negotiations, multi-signing scenarios or live database edits.
